About CJKmap

A visual map for Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

CJKmap brings together maps for Chinese Hanzi, Japanese Kanji, and Korean Hangul, making it easier to explore each language's writing system.

The Kanji Map is available now. Hanzi and Hangul maps are still in development.
